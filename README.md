# FEHB Community Survey 2026: Results

**See the results: https://andrewbyrley.com/fehb/**

In October 2026, 80 Foreign Service households answered an anonymous survey about their FEHB health plans. This repo holds the results page and the survey data. Both were posted ahead of 2027 Open Season (Nov 9 to Dec 14).

## What's in this repo

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | The results page. It shows what members said about value, 2027 plans, overseas vs. U.S. care, claims, and other plans. |
| `fehb-survey-2026-responses.csv` | The survey responses, with one row per household. |
| `og-image.png` | The preview image that shows when the link is shared on social media. |

## About the survey

- **Who answered:** 80 households responded to a Google Form shared in Foreign Service Facebook groups between Oct 8 and Oct 31, 2026.
- **Mostly FSBP:** 66 of the 80 households are on the Foreign Service Benefit Plan. GEHA HDHP (8) and Blue Cross (6) results reflect only a handful of households each.
- **Not a random sample:** people chose to respond, so the results describe the people who answered, not all FEHB members.
- **Time frame:** questions about claims and service asked about the past 2 years. Plans change, so past experience may not predict 2027.

## About the data file

- Each column is one survey question, using the question's wording as its header.
- A blank cell means the question didn't apply or was skipped. For example, only people who filed their own overseas claims saw the claims questions.
- To protect respondents, timestamps, comments, and write-in answers were removed, and the rows were shuffled. No names or email addresses were ever collected.
- Please don't try to identify individual respondents.

## Using the data with an AI assistant

You can upload the CSV to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another assistant and ask about households like yours. For 2027 costs, add these:

- [2027 premiums for nationwide plans](https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/healthcare/plan-information/premiums/py2027/fehb/ffs.xlsx) (OPM, Excel)
- Each plan's 2027 brochure, through [OPM's plan comparison tool](https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/healthcare/plan-information/compare-plans/)

The results page includes a starter prompt you can copy.
